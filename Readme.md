# 📊 Samsung Galaxy Sales Performance Dashboard

## 👋 About this project
This project is a Power BI dashboard built to analyze Samsung Galaxy sales performance across different regions, customer groups, and product categories. The goal is to understand sales trends, customer behavior, and overall business performance in a simple and visual way.

I created this dashboard as part of my data analytics practice to improve my skills in Power BI, data visualization, and storytelling with data.

---

## 📌 What this dashboard shows
This dashboard gives a complete overview of:

- Total sales performance and revenue
- Units sold and pricing insights
- Monthly sales trends
- Country-wise sales distribution
- Category-wise and age-wise customer analysis
- Payment method preferences
- Customer ratings
- 5G vs Non-5G product comparison

---

## 📊 Key Insights
Some interesting findings from the dashboard:

- Sales fluctuate across months, with noticeable peaks in mid-year
- EMI and digital payment methods are commonly used by customers
- Asia contributes a major share of overall sales
- Certain product categories perform significantly better across age groups
- Customer ratings are mostly in the "Good" and "Average" range
